# Hospital workbench adaptation

Mode: Operate. Existing identity: Integrate hospital workbench. Implementation path: code, selected by the user.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Put hospital catalog discovery and the next governance action in view. Sign-in belongs to the Integrate employee account and application portal.

OWN-WORLD: Integrate green accent and tinted neutral surfaces; its Chinese system font stack; compact navigation, consistently sized controls, native OpenMetadata core components. No marketing carousel or decorative data graphics.

STORY: Staff log in once to Integrate, select the data governance application, and receive their own OpenMetadata governance permissions. They search assets, inspect definitions and open quality incidents.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Portal sign-in presents a left-aligned title and one clear portal action, with connection progress or recovery in the same region. The home surface presents search first, four actual catalog totals, an initial sample of catalog assets and governance actions. Asset rows follow the catalog API's order; they make no recency claim.

FORM: Extend an existing hospital product identity and the existing metadata workflows. No concept roll: these are scoped surfaces with specified tasks and an incumbent component system. Taste dials: variance 3, motion 2, density 5, reflecting a hospital operating environment.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
